About Bazem

Our Story

We believe good design is felt before it's seen.

Bazem has spent more than 45 years developing homes, apartments and communities across New South Wales and Victoria. What began as a small family building practice has grown into a considered development house — one that still works project by project, site by site, rather than chasing scale for its own sake.

We partner with the region's most respected architects, interior and landscape designers. It's this depth of collaboration, held over decades, that lets us build with a genuine point of view rather than a formula.
